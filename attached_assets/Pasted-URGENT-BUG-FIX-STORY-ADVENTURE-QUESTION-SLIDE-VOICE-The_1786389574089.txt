URGENT BUG FIX — STORY ADVENTURE QUESTION SLIDE VOICE

There is a critical bug in the Story Adventure voice system.

Current behavior:
- Slide 1 voice works.
- Slide 2 voice works.
- Slide 3 voice works.
- When the user reaches Slide 4, which is the QUESTION / CHOICE slide, the voice stops working.
- The question is visible on screen, but the AI voice does NOT speak the question/options.
- The UI also shows the voice error/retry message.
- This must be fixed without breaking the working voice on previous slides.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT replace Gemini voice with browser SpeechSynthesis.
Do NOT introduce another TTS provider.
Do NOT create a second robotic voice system.
The Story Adventure must use the EXISTING Gemini voice implementation consistently.

==================================================

"Option 2 — Aman ke liye help bulao."

The order MUST always be:

QUESTION
↓
OPTION 1
↓
OPTION 2

==================================================

The question and options should NOT be fired as three independent simultaneous audio requests.

speak(question)

==================================================

After the question + both options have been spoken:

Wait for the user to select an answer.

After another 5 seconds, say a DIFFERENT reminder.

option 1 finished
+
option 2 finished.
