Q: "React kyun? Android app kyun nahi banaya?"

"Reach. A browser app runs on school computer labs, shared family phones, anything — no install, no Play Store account, no storage space needed. For a child-safety tool, removing the install barrier IS the distribution strategy."

Q: "Phaser kya hai? Unity kyun nahi?"

"Phaser is a web-native 2D game engine — open source, no licensing, loads in seconds. Unity WebGL builds are 30–50MB+ and need powerful devices; Phaser runs smoothly on low-end phones, which is exactly rural India's hardware."

Q: "TypeScript kyun?"

"Legal content is safety-critical — TypeScript catches content-structure mistakes at compile time, before a child ever sees them. Every quiz, scene, and helpline entry is type-checked."

Q: "html2canvas + jsPDF ka kya role hai?"

"Certificates generate entirely in the browser — bilingual, with Hindi fonts embedded. Nothing goes to a server, so a certificate never carries personal data anywhere."

BACKEND line se
Q: "Express 5 hi kyun? / Backend itna simple kyun?"

"Deliberately boring. One stateless Express server, Zod validation on every input, structured logging. For child safety you want a backend you can audit in an afternoon."

Q: "pnpm monorepo kyun?"

"Frontend, backend and design system share one repo — shared types mean the safety contract between client and server can't silently drift."

AI AND ML line se (sabse zyada drilling yahin hogi)
Q: "Model fine-tune kyun nahi kiya?"

"Fine-tuning bakes knowledge invisibly into weights — you can't audit it. Our curated India Code corpus is a reviewable text file: every legal fact the AI cites, a human approved. Grounding beats fine-tuning for auditability."

Q: "Gemini Flash kyun, Pro/GPT-4 kyun nahi?"

"Children ask short questions and need fast answers — Flash gives low latency at a fraction of the cost, and corpus grounding covers the knowledge. We pay for speed and safety, not for size we don't need."

Q: "Do alag TTS kyun — Gemini TTS aur Sarvam dono?"

"Different jobs. Story narration is pre-scripted, so Gemini TTS gives cinematic quality there. Live voice chat needs an Indian-language specialist with fast turnaround — that's Sarvam. Right tool per job."

Q: "Streaming chat mein safety kaise? Chunk toh nikal jayega."

"The gate runs on the accumulated reply before every chunk is forwarded — the moment anything trips, the stream stops. A child can never see an ungated word."

Q: "Curated corpus kitna bada hai / kaise banaya?"

"Hand-reviewed summaries of the seven acts we teach, written at child reading level, with real section citations from India Code. Small by design — we'd rather cover seven laws perfectly than five hundred badly."

API SERVICES / DATABASE / AUTH lines se

"That's an opt-in layer, not a redesign. The stateless core stays; a school edition can add aggregated, consented analytics on top. We started from zero data because you can always add collection with consent — you can never un-leak data you shouldn't have had."
